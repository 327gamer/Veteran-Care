PRIORITY CATEGORY EXPANSION — SUBCATEGORY SYSTEM

GOAL:
Replicate the “Disabled Veterans” subcategory flow across the highest-impact categories to improve navigation, engagement, and conversion.

--------------------------------------------------
TIER 1 (IMPLEMENT NEXT — HIGHEST IMPACT)
--------------------------------------------------

1. Mental Health
WHY:
- High urgency category
- Users often don’t know what type of help they need
- Perfect for guided navigation

SUBCATEGORIES:
- PTSD & Trauma Support
- Crisis & Suicide Prevention
- Substance Abuse & Addiction
- Counseling & Therapy
- Inpatient / Outpatient Treatment
- Peer Support Groups
- Family Support (Mental Health)

--------------------------------------------------

2. Housing & Home Services
WHY:
- One of the most common needs
- Users are often in very different situations

SUBCATEGORIES:
- Emergency Housing / Homeless Shelters
- Rental Assistance
- Home Ownership Programs
- VA Housing Benefits
- Home Modifications (Accessibility)
- Transitional Housing
- Foreclosure Prevention

--------------------------------------------------

3. Employment Support
WHY:
- Strong long-term value category
- Key for reintegration and independence

SUBCATEGORIES:
- Job Placement Programs
- Resume & Career Coaching
- Vocational Rehabilitation
- Apprenticeships & Skilled Trades
- Veteran-Friendly Employers
- Entrepreneurship & Small Business Support
- DVOP / Workforce Programs

--------------------------------------------------

  → back navigation
