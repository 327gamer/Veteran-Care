PRODUCTS:

-------------------------------------

Each listing should support:

- business_name
- category
- group_type:
  - service
  - product
- location / geo support
- listing_type:
  - discount
  - lead
  - hybrid (optional later, no need to fully build now)
- discount_value (optional)
- discount_description (optional)
- description
- contact_method
- CTA label
- Connect / Claim Offer behavior

IMPORTANT:
Keep “group_type” and “listing_type” as separate concepts.

- search inclusion
- future reporting
