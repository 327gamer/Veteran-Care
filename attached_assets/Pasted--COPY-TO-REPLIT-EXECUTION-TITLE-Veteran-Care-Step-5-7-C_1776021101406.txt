IMPLEMENTATION PLAN

STEP 1 — PARTNER PERFORMANCE FLAGS (DERIVED)

Using:
GET /api/admin/intelligence/partner-performance

Add derived field:

partner_performance_status

Logic:

- strong → response_rate ≥ 40
- moderate → 15–39
- weak → 1–14
- inactive → 0

DO NOT store in DB.
Compute dynamically.

-------------------------------------

STEP 2 — CATEGORY PERFORMANCE FLAGS (DERIVED)

Using:
GET /api/admin/intelligence/category-performance

Add:

category_performance_status

Logic:

- high_value → billable > 0 AND paid > 0
- emerging → high volume + paid = 0
- low_conversion → billable > 0 AND paid < 5%
- inactive → leads exist but billable = 0

-------------------------------------

STEP 3 — PERFORMANCE ALERT FLAGS

Category:
- high volume + low payment → LOW CONVERSION
- no billable leads → NO MONETIZATION
