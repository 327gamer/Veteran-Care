PART 1 — 72-HOUR FAILSAFE REASSIGNMENT
-------------------------------------

BUSINESS RULE:

IF:
- response_status = "pending"
- AND current_time > assigned_at + 72 hours

THEN:
- reassign lead to next eligible entity
- send new email
- log reassignment

- assigned_at exists
- NOT accepted
