Founder review:

Georgia Phase 5 progress is strong and appreciated.

However, we need to refine the expansion philosophy now before Florida and future national rollout.

This is important:

We should NOT be setting arbitrary row ceilings like 650, 750, or 1,000 as if every state is the same.

Each state is different.

Examples:

- Georgia may support hundreds or 1,000+ quality rows.
- Florida may support several thousand.
- Texas may support several thousand.
- California may support many thousands.
- Wyoming or Vermont may support far fewer.

So the goal moving forward is NOT to hit a random number.

The goal is:

MAXIMUM QUALITY COVERAGE BY STATE

Meaning:

Add as many legitimate, useful, verified resources as reasonably possible through structured phases until the state is operationally strong with minimal meaningful gaps.

--------------------------------------------------
NEW NATIONAL EXPANSION PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------

Every state should be expanded in phases until coverage is mature.

Some states may be complete in 4 phases.

Some states may need 6 phases.

Some very large states may need 7+ phases.

That is acceptable.

We are building a national system, not forcing every state into the same row count.

--------------------------------------------------
UPDATE NATIONAL SOP NOW
--------------------------------------------------

Please update the rollout model immediately.

Base model:

Phase 1 — Major Metro Foundation
Phase 2 — Secondary Cities + Statewide Programs
Phase 3 — Small Town + Rural Coverage
Phase 4 — Gold Standard Completion / Optimization
Phase 5 — Saturation / Metro + Category Depth
Phase 6 — Final QA / Micro-Fill / Ceiling Check

Additional rule:

If a state still has meaningful gaps after Phase 6, continue with:

Phase 7+
Specialized Completion Waves

Examples:

- large county backfills
- deep metro passes
- niche category passes
- university systems
- tribal / military / coastal / agricultural regions
- additional nonprofit layers
- chapter/post networks
- faith-based verified layers
- transit networks
- hospital system sub-programs

A state is complete when coverage is strong and meaningful gaps are minimal — not when a phase number ends.
