VeteranCare.com – Priority UX / Automation / Contact System Upgrade Request

We reviewed the latest live mobile screenshots in detail. Strong progress overall. The platform is improving significantly. However, we now need to shift from “website pages” into a scalable automated operating system.

This next phase must prioritize:
1. Automation
2. Reduced admin workload
3. Premium branding consistency
4. AI-first support flow
5. Scalable lead handling
6. Professional SaaS-grade user experience

==================================================
SECTION 1 – CONTACT PAGE MUST BECOME AI-FIRST
==================================================

Current issue:
The Contact Us page still feels like a traditional company contact form. This invites unnecessary manual emails and creates the wrong expectation of live support.

We do NOT want:
- general support inbox overload
- endless random questions
- users expecting live chat agents
- dependency on manual replies
- admin becoming customer service

Required new model:

PRIMARY CTA:
“Talk to Veteran Navigator Now”

When clicked:
Open AI Navigator chat drawer / bottom chat panel instantly.

AI should begin guided intake flow such as:

1. What do you need help with?
2. Are you a veteran, spouse, caregiver, family member, or organization?
3. What city / state are you in?
4. Is this urgent?
5. Which category best fits your need?

AI should attempt to solve / route 80%+ of requests before any email form is shown.

Only after AI cannot solve should escalation/contact form appear.

==================================================
SECTION 2 – REMOVE ALL “LIVE PERSON” LANGUAGE
==================================================

Current wording creates bad expectations.

Remove phrases such as:
“A live person follows up when needed”

Replace with approved alternatives:

Option A:
“Special requests may be reviewed by our team when necessary”

Option B:
“Complex matters may be escalated for internal review”

Option C:
“Urgent partnership, media, legal, or critical veteran matters may be reviewed”

Important:
Never promise live agents, phone reps, or instant humans.

==================================================
SECTION 3 – CONTACT FORM SHOULD ONLY HANDLE HIGH VALUE INQUIRIES
==================================================

Current dropdown allows too many low-value messages.

Remove consumer/general categories.

New dropdown options only:

- Partnership Inquiry
- Trusted Partner Application Support
- Media Request
- Legal Inquiry
- Government / Agency Collaboration
- Sponsorship Opportunity
- Report Incorrect Listing
- Technical Website Issue
- Billing / Subscription Issue
- Data / Privacy Request

Do NOT include:
- General Question
- Need Help
- Benefits Help
- Housing Help
- Employment Help
- Resource Search

Those must be routed to AI Navigator.

==================================================

==================================================

Desired final flow:

User clicks Contact

STEP 1:

STEP 2:
AI answers questions, routes pages, gives links, suggests categories
