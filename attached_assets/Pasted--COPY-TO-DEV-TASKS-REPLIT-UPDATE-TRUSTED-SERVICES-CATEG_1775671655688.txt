Clean up the category architecture so that:

--------------------------------------------------
MENTAL HEALTH DECISION
--------------------------------------------------

Keep Mental Health as its own MAIN category in Regular Resources.

Do NOT move Mental Health under Healthcare.

Reason:
- it is a major high-intent category
- it has its own strong subcategory system already
- it needs to remain visible and easy to access
- it should stay separate from general healthcare navigation

So going forward:

Regular Resources should have BOTH:
- Mental Health
- Healthcare

as separate top-level categories.
