TITLE:
Veteran Care – Step 7.7 – Partner Confidence / Communication Layer

PRIORITY:
HIGH (Retention + Monetization Protection Layer)

-------------------------------------
CLARIFICATION
-------------------------------------

This step is:

✅ Step 7.7 – Partner Communication Layer  
❌ NOT routing changes  
❌ NOT billing changes  
❌ NOT Stripe changes  
❌ NOT automation  

This step improves HOW the system communicates with partners.

-------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE
-------------------------------------

Reduce partner confusion and prevent disputes by:

- clearly explaining how leads are distributed  
- clarifying why lead volume may vary  
- reinforcing fairness and system logic  
- aligning partner expectations with system behavior  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 1 — LEAD DELIVERY EXPLANATION
-------------------------------------

Add a clearly visible section in the Partner Transparency panel:

Explain:

- how leads are assigned  
- what determines eligibility  
- why a partner may or may not receive a lead  

Example messaging:

“Leads are assigned based on category, location, and availability.  
You receive leads when you match the user’s request and are eligible at that time.”

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 2 — FAIRNESS EXPLANATION BLOCK
-------------------------------------

Add a persistent explanation block:

“Veteran Care uses a fair rotation system to distribute leads evenly across eligible partners.  
No manual favoritism is applied.”

This should be clearly visible (not hidden).

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 3 — VARIABILITY EXPLANATION
-------------------------------------

Add a section explaining fluctuations:

“Lead volume may vary due to:
- demand in your category  
- geographic matching  
- timing of requests  
- partner availability”

This is critical for expectation management.

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 4 — “WHY AM I SEEING THIS?” TOOLTIP
-------------------------------------

Add a small info icon or tooltip near fairness metrics.

Tooltip should explain:

- why the partner is seeing their current share  
- that distribution depends on eligibility + timing  

Keep short and simple.

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 5 — PARTNER EXPECTATION GUIDANCE
-------------------------------------

Add short advisory guidance (non-technical):

Examples:

- “Staying active helps ensure you receive eligible leads”  
- “Responding quickly helps you get the most value from the platform”  
- “Consistent availability improves your participation”

IMPORTANT:
- This is guidance only  
- NOT tied to routing logic  
- NOT presented as a requirement or rule  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 6 — TONE + LANGUAGE
-------------------------------------

STRICT RULE:

Language must be:
- clear  
- non-technical  
- neutral and non-defensive  
- trust-building  

Avoid:
- internal terminology  
- system jargon  
- anything that sounds like justification or excuse  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 7 — NO DATA EXPANSION
-------------------------------------

Do NOT add:

- new metrics  
- new calculations  
- new backend logic  
- new endpoints  

This is a communication layer only.

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 8 — UI STRUCTURE
-------------------------------------

Place inside Partner Transparency panel.

Use:

- clear sections  
- short text blocks  
- optional collapsible sections  
- good spacing for readability  

-------------------------------------
TEST CASES REQUIRED
-------------------------------------

Dev must validate:

1. All explanation text renders correctly  
2. Tooltip displays correctly  
3. No new data exposure  
4. No performance impact  
5. No regression to routing, billing, or Stripe  

-------------------------------------
DELIVERABLE
-------------------------------------

- communication layer implemented  
- explanation text visible  
- tooltips working  
- guidance text added  
- validation complete  
- no regression introduced

END
