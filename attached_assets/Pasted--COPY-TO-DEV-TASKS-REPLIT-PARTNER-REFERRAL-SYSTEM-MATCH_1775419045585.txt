### PARTNER REFERRAL SYSTEM – MATCH VETERAN REFERRAL SYSTEM (NO FORMS)

We are NOT building a manual referral form system.

We are building a referral system for Trusted Partners that works EXACTLY like the existing Veteran referral system — but for businesses instead of users.

-------------------------------------
CORE REQUIREMENT
-------------------------------------

The Partner Referral System must be:

- 100% LINK-BASED (NO forms)
- fast, simple, mobile-friendly
- fully trackable
- identical UX to the current veteran referral system

-------------------------------------
PARTNER EXPERIENCE (AFTER APPROVAL)
-------------------------------------

Once a business is approved as a Trusted Partner and logs in, they must see:

### "Refer a Business & Earn Free Months"

This page/section should mirror the current user referral screen.

-------------------------------------
FEATURES REQUIRED
-------------------------------------

1. UNIQUE REFERRAL LINK

Each partner gets a unique referral link:

Example:
https://veterancare.com/partners/apply?ref=PARTNER_ID

Must include:
- Copy button
- Visible input field with link

-------------------------------------

2. PRE-WRITTEN SHARE MESSAGES (VERY IMPORTANT)

We need message templates (same as veteran system style):

Tabs:
- Friendly
- Professional
- Direct

Each message must include:
- short introduction
- explanation of Veteran Care
- referral link auto-inserted

Example:

"We’ve partnered with Veteran Care to help veterans connect with trusted services. If your business works with veterans or wants to, I highly recommend checking this out: [REFERRAL LINK]"

Buttons:
- Copy Message
- SMS
- Email
- WhatsApp

This removes the need for partners to “sell” — they just send.

-------------------------------------

3. TRACKING DASHBOARD

Display:

- Referrals count
- Free months earned
- Pending rewards
- (optional) conversion status

-------------------------------------

4. LEADERBOARD SYSTEM (IMPORTANT ADDITION)

Add a partner leaderboard similar to the referral system:

- Top referring partners
- Ranked by successful business referrals
- Visible inside partner referral section

This drives engagement and competition.

-------------------------------------

5. CREDIT LOGIC (NO CHANGE)

Keep existing backend logic:

- referred business must sign up
- must complete first billing cycle
- then apply 100% credit to referring partner’s next invoice

-------------------------------------

6. SIGNUP PAGE (ALREADY DONE – CONFIRM)

Keep referral explanation on partner signup page.

Ensure this line is present:

"Once approved, you’ll gain access to your partner referral dashboard where you can share your referral link and track your rewards."

-------------------------------------

IMPORTANT:

DO NOT BUILD:
- manual referral forms
- admin-driven referral entry

This must be:
- link-driven
- automated
- scalable
- identical to user referral UX

-------------------------------------

GOAL:

Make referrals:
- instant
- frictionless
- shareable in 1 click
- trackable
- scalable for growth
