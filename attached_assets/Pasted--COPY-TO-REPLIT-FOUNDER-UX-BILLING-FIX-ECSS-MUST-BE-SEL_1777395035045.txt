FOUNDER UX/BILLING FIX — ECSS MUST BE SELECTABLE + UPDATE SUMMARY

Good progress: the Elite Category Sponsor Slot card now appears in the Trusted Partner signup flow.

But it is still not working correctly as a selectable paid upgrade.

==================================================
CURRENT ISSUE
==================================================

The ECSS card is visible, but:

1. There is no clear checkbox / toggle to select it.
2. It does not add $499/month to the Monthly Summary.
3. It does not automatically activate Direct Lead Delivery.
4. It does not make clear that $49.99 per accepted lead is required.
5. The card says “Choose one of those categories above,” but there is no action button/toggle for the user to confirm they want ECSS.

This is confusing for trusted partners.

==================================================
REQUIRED USER FLOW
==================================================

A trusted partner should be able to:

1. Choose base plan:
   - State Plan $99/month
   OR
   - National Plan $499/month

2. Choose state.

3. Choose service category / specialty.

4. See Elite Category Sponsor Slot section.

5. If eligible and available, they must be able to select it clearly using a checkbox/toggle:

   “Yes, I want to claim the Elite Category Sponsor Slot”

6. When selected:

   - Add $499/month to Monthly Summary
   - Automatically turn ON Direct Lead Delivery
   - Show that accepted leads are billed at $49.99 each
   - Reveal required ECSS fields:
     - logo upload
     - business display name
     - banner headline / CTA
     - lead destination email

7. If ECSS is deselected:
   - remove $499/month from summary
   - do NOT force Direct Lead Delivery unless user selected it separately

==================================================
BILLING RULE
==================================================

ECSS requires:

Base Plan:
- $99 State Plan
OR
- $499 National Plan

PLUS:

Elite Category Sponsor Slot:
- $499/month

PLUS:

Direct Lead Delivery:
- $49.99 per accepted qualified lead

When ECSS is selected, Direct Lead Delivery must automatically activate because the whole point of ECSS is direct lead capture.

==================================================
MONTHLY SUMMARY MUST SHOW
==================================================

Example with State Plan + ECSS:

State Plan (base)        $99.00/mo
Elite Sponsor Slot       $499.00/mo
Total                    $598.00/mo

Below summary, show note:

“Accepted qualified leads are billed separately at $49.99 per lead.”

Do not hide this.

==================================================
DIRECT LEAD DELIVERY CARD
==================================================

If ECSS is selected:

- Direct Lead Delivery toggle should turn ON automatically.
- It can show as enabled because ECSS requires it.
- The card should clearly state:
  “Required for Elite Sponsor Slots.”

Do not let ECSS be selected while direct leads are off.

==================================================
VISUAL / UX RULES
==================================================

Make ECSS look like a premium paid upgrade similar to Featured Partner Placement and Near Me Boost, but more important.

Use:

- checkbox or switch
- strong “Limited Availability” language
- clear price
- clear lead-fee notice
- visible selected state
- monthly summary update

It should be simple enough for a non-technical business owner to understand immediately.

==================================================
DO NOT
==================================================

- Do not make ECSS passive information only.
- Do not require users to guess how to claim it.
- Do not leave it out of Monthly Summary.
- Do not allow ECSS without Direct Lead Delivery.
- Do not break normal $99 / $499 signup path.
