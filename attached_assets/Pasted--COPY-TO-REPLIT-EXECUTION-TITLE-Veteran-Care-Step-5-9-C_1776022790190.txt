STEP 4 — REVENUE SIGNAL FLAGS
-------------------------------------

Add derived revenue signals.

For partners:
- high_revenue
- low_revenue
- no_revenue

For categories:
- monetizing
- underperforming
- non_monetizing

Simple initial logic:
- no_revenue = 0 paid leads
- low_revenue = low paid % relative to volume
- high_revenue = at least 1+ paid leads with consistent response
- monetizing = revenue exists
- non_monetizing = leads exist but no paid revenue
- underperforming = activity exists but weak monetization

Keep this lightweight and adjustable later.
