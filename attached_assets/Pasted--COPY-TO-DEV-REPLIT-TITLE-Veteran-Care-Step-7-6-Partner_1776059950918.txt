TITLE:
Veteran Care – Step 7.6 – Partner Transparency Layer

PRIORITY:
HIGH (Trust + Retention + Monetization Layer)

-------------------------------------
CLARIFICATION
-------------------------------------

This step is:

✅ Step 7.6 – Partner Transparency  
❌ NOT routing changes  
❌ NOT billing changes  
❌ NOT Stripe changes  
❌ NOT automation  

This step exposes limited, privacy-protected fairness data to partners.

-------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE
-------------------------------------

Allow partners to:

- understand how leads are distributed  
- see their own performance within a scope  
- trust the fairness of the system  
- reduce disputes proactively  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 1 — PARTNER-SCOPED VIEW
-------------------------------------

Create a partner-facing view (admin-only for now, filtered by partner_id):

Show ONLY:

- total leads received  
- percentage share  
- expected share  
- deviation from expected share  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 2 — STRICT PRIVACY RULES
-------------------------------------

Partners MUST NOT see:

- other partner names  
- other partner lead counts  
- full distribution breakdown  

Partners MAY see:

- total number of partners in the scope  
- expected share (e.g., “50% with 2 partners”)  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 3 — FAIRNESS EXPLANATION
-------------------------------------

Include simple explanation text:

- “Leads are distributed evenly across eligible partners.”  
- “Your share reflects fair rotation based on availability.”  
- “Small variations can occur due to timing and availability.”  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 4 — TRUST INDICATOR
-------------------------------------

Display visible trust badges:

- FAIR DISTRIBUTION ACTIVE  
- ROTATION SYSTEM ENABLED  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 5 — LIGHT TREND INDICATOR
-------------------------------------

Show simple trend:

- improving  
- stable  
- slight_skew  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 6 — STRICT INTERNAL DATA PROTECTION
-------------------------------------

Do NOT expose:

- advisory flags  
- persistent imbalance  
- fairness history snapshots  
- admin-only intervention signals  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 7 — UI SIMPLICITY
-------------------------------------

Use simple layout:

- card or panel  
- clean, readable  
- non-technical language  

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 8 — DELIVERY FORMAT
-------------------------------------

Implement inside admin panel with partner filter OR lightweight endpoint.

-------------------------------------
TEST CASES REQUIRED
-------------------------------------

1. Partner sees only their own data  
2. Expected share correct  
3. Deviation calculation correct  
4. No other partner data exposed  
5. Explanation text visible  
6. No regression  

-------------------------------------
DELIVERABLE
-------------------------------------

- partner transparency view implemented  
- privacy rules enforced  
- explanation + trust indicators visible  
- validation complete  
- no regression introduced  

-------------------------------------
END
