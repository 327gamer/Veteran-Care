TITLE:
Veteran Care – Step 7.9 – Partner Retention / Success Layer

PRIORITY:
HIGH (Retention + Monetization Protection Layer)

-------------------------------------
CLARIFICATION
-------------------------------------

This step is:

✅ Step 7.9 – Partner Retention Layer  
❌ NOT routing changes  
❌ NOT billing changes  
❌ NOT Stripe changes  
❌ NOT automation  

This step introduces early retention signals and success guidance for partners.

-------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE
-------------------------------------

Help partners:

- understand if they are on a healthy path
- recognize early warning signs
- stay engaged before frustration builds
- increase long-term retention

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 1 — PARTNER HEALTH STATUS
-------------------------------------

Add a simple partner-facing status:

- Healthy
- At Risk
- Needs Attention

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 2 — HEALTH LOGIC (LIGHTWEIGHT)
-------------------------------------

Use existing data only.

Signals may include:
- activity level
- responsiveness trend
- recent engagement

Suggested logic:

Healthy:
- Activity Level = Active
- AND Responsiveness Trend = Stable or Improving

At Risk:
- Activity Level = Moderate
- OR Responsiveness Trend = Needs Attention

Needs Attention:
- Activity Level = Low
- AND responsiveness is weak / inconsistent based on existing indicators

Keep logic lightweight and explainable.
Do NOT introduce scoring or new backend complexity unless absolutely necessary.

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 3 — STATUS EXPLANATION
-------------------------------------

Each status must include simple explanation text.

Examples:

Healthy:
“You are actively engaging and in a strong position.”

At Risk:
“Your engagement has slowed. Staying active will help maintain opportunities.”

Needs Attention:
“Your activity is currently low. Increasing engagement will help you get more value from the platform.”

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 4 — SUCCESS GUIDANCE BLOCK
-------------------------------------

Add a section:

→ “How to Stay Successful”

Include simple guidance such as:
- respond quickly
- stay active
- maintain availability
- engage consistently

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 5 — POSITIVE, NON-PUNITIVE TONE
-------------------------------------

STRICT RULE:

- no penalties implied
- no warnings framed as threats
- no ranking or comparison
- no shame-based language

Tone must be:
- supportive
- encouraging
- constructive

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 6 — NO SYSTEM CHANGE
-------------------------------------

Do NOT:
- affect routing
- affect billing
- change fairness logic
- introduce scoring system
- add partner comparisons

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 7 — UI PLACEMENT
-------------------------------------

Place inside Partner Transparency panel:

- below performance section (Step 7.8)
- clearly labeled as “Your Status”

Keep layout clean and easy to understand.

-------------------------------------
TEST CASES REQUIRED
-------------------------------------

1. Status displays correctly
2. Logic aligns with existing activity data
3. Explanation text renders correctly
4. No partner comparison leakage
5. No performance impact
6. No regression to routing, billing, or Stripe

-------------------------------------
DELIVERABLE
-------------------------------------

- partner health status visible
- explanation text included
- success guidance included
- validation complete
- no regression introduced

END
