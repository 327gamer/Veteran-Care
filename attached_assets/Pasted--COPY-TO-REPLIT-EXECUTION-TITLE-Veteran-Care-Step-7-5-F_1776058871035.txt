This step packages existing fairness + history data into clear, dispute-ready evidence views.

-------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE
-------------------------------------

Allow admin to quickly answer:

- Is this partner being treated fairly?
- What share of leads have they received?
- Has distribution been consistent over time?
- Is this a temporary skew or real imbalance?

This is about presenting proof clearly, not just storing data.

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 1 — PARTNER FAIRNESS VIEW
-------------------------------------

For each partner inside a scope show:

- total_rotated_leads_received
- percentage_share
- expected_share
- deviation_from_expected

Calculation:

expected_share = 100 / number_of_partners

deviation = percentage_share - expected_share

Display example:

Expected: 50%  
Actual: 66.7%  
Deviation: +16.7%

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 2 — FAIRNESS SUMMARY BLOCK
-------------------------------------

At top of scope panel display:

- total_rotated_leads
- number_of_partners
- expected_share
- fairness_status
- advisory_flag
- trend_direction
- persistent_imbalance

This must be readable in one compact block.

-------------------------------------
REQUIREMENT 3 — EXPLAINABILITY TEXT
-------------------------------------

Add plain-English explanation based on conditions:

balanced:
→ “Distribution is within acceptable range.”

slight_skew:
→ “Slight skew detected but within tolerance.”

imbalance_detected:
→ “Distribution shows imbalance in current assignments.”

persistent_imbalance:
→ “Persistent imbalance detected — review recommended.”

low_sample:
→ “Insufficient data for fairness assessment.”
