CHUNK 4 – LEAD ROUTING ENGINE (CLARIFICATION)

Good question.

We are NOT building a new lead system.

We are extending the existing system to support monetization-ready routing.

-------------------------------------
CURRENT SYSTEM (KEEP AS-IS)
-------------------------------------

You already have:
- lead creation (Get Help / Navigator)
- admin lead view
- manual routing
- email notifications
- partner management

Do NOT rebuild or replace these.

-------------------------------------
GOAL OF CHUNK 4
-------------------------------------

Introduce controlled, rule-based lead routing using:

- lead-eligible categories (from Chunk 1)
- is_lead_enabled flag (from Chunk 2)

This ensures leads are only routed to partners who:
- are in the correct category
- have opted into Direct Lead Delivery

-------------------------------------
WHAT TO BUILD (ONLY THIS)
-------------------------------------

1. LEAD ROUTING FILTER

When a lead is created:

System should:
- identify category_slug
- identify subcategory_slug (if present)
- identify location (if available)

Then:

Find partners where:
- category matches
- is_lead_enabled = true
- partner is active (already exists)

-------------------------------------

2. DELIVERY PREPARATION (NO BILLING)

When eligible partners are found:

Prepare lead for delivery:
- attach partner_id(s)
- mark delivery_status = "ready_for_delivery"

Do NOT:
- charge anything
- modify Stripe
- change billing logic

-------------------------------------

3. DELIVERY EXECUTION (KEEP SIMPLE)

Use EXISTING email system.

If routing is valid:
- send lead to matched partner(s)

-------------------------------------

4. EVENT LOGGING INTEGRATION

For every routed lead:

Create/update lead_events entry:

- event_type = lead_routed
- lead_class = explicit_lead
- category_slug
- partner_id
- delivery_status = delivered

-------------------------------------

5. FALLBACK LOGIC

If NO eligible partners found:

- do NOT break flow
- keep existing behavior:
  - admin/manual routing
  - resource fallback
  - AI assistance

-------------------------------------
