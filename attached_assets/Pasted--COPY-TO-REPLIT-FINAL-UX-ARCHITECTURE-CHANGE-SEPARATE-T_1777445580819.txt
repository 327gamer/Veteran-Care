🚨 FINAL UX + ARCHITECTURE CHANGE — SEPARATE TRUSTED PARTNER AND ELITE SERVICE PARTNER FLOWS

We are NOT continuing with the current approach of burying Elite inside the existing Trusted Partner signup form.

We are separating the products clearly.

This is the final direction.

==================================================
CORE DECISION
==================================================

We now have TWO clear signup paths:

1. Trusted Services Partner  
2. Elite Service Partner

Use the name:

👑 Elite Service Partner

NOT “Elite Sponsor.”

==================================================
HOMEPAGE / TRUSTED SERVICES ENTRY UPDATE
==================================================

Add TWO separate boxes:

----------------------------------------
BOX 1 — Become a Trusted Services Partner
----------------------------------------

Existing flow.

Keep current system:

- $99 State Plan
- $499 National Plan
- Featured Partner Placement
- Near Me Boost
- Optional Direct Lead Delivery
- Multi-category / multi-subcategory services

CTA:
Apply as Trusted Partner

----------------------------------------
BOX 2 — Become an Elite Service Partner
----------------------------------------

New premium flow.

Messaging:

“Own the top placement in your selected state, category, or subcategory. Only one Elite Service Partner slot is available per market. Receive direct, high-intent leads from veterans and families.”

CTA:
Apply for Elite Placement

Route:
/elite-partner-apply

==================================================
NEW PAGE: /elite-partner-apply
==================================================

Create a separate Elite Service Partner application page.

Do NOT keep patching the old /partner-apply form.

Use similar styling and structure, but this must be its own clean premium flow.

==================================================
ELITE SERVICE PARTNER FLOW
==================================================

Order MUST be:

1. Select Base Plan
   - State Plan $99/month
   OR
   - National Plan $499/month

2. Select State
   - Required if State Plan
   - If National Plan, still allow state selection for the Elite slot because Elite slots are state/category/subcategory specific

3. Select Category

4. Select Subcategory

5. System checks availability

6. User selects ONE Elite slot

7. Monthly Summary updates

8. Business info / branding fields

9. Submit application / Stripe checkout

==================================================
BASE PLAN REQUIRED
==================================================

Elite Service Partner is NOT free-standing.

They must still choose a base plan:

- $99/month State Plan
OR
- $499/month National Plan

Then Elite is added on top:

- + $499/month Elite Service Partner Slot
- + $49.99 per accepted qualified lead

==================================================
MONTHLY SUMMARY REQUIRED
==================================================

The Elite page must include a Monthly Summary before checkout.

Example:

State Plan                    $99.00/mo  
Elite Service Partner Slot    $499.00/mo  
-----------------------------------------  
Total                         $598.00/mo  

Also show:

“Accepted qualified leads are billed separately at $49.99 per accepted lead.”

If National Plan selected:

National Plan                 $499.00/mo  
Elite Service Partner Slot    $499.00/mo  
-----------------------------------------  
Total                         $998.00/mo  

==================================================
CATEGORY + SUBCATEGORY SLOT SYSTEM
==================================================

Do NOT limit Elite to only 4 categories.

Use the full existing category / subcategory system.

Businesses should choose:

- State
- Category
- Subcategory

Then the system checks what Elite slots are available.

Example:

State: South Carolina  
Category: Financial & Credit Services  
Subcategory: Mortgages  

Available slot:

South Carolina > Financial & Credit Services > Mortgages

==================================================
SINGLE SLOT SELECTION
==================================================

This is NOT the same as service specialties.

Trusted Partners can select multiple services.

Elite Service Partners must select ONLY ONE Elite slot per application.

Use:

- radio button
OR
- single-select dropdown

Do NOT use multi-checkbox behavior for Elite slots.

==================================================
AVAILABILITY LOGIC
==================================================

After state + category + subcategory are selected:

Show:

Available Elite Service Partner Slots

Examples:

- Mortgages — Available
- VA Loans — Sold Out
- Refinancing — Available

If available:
Allow selection.

If sold:
Show SOLD OUT + Join Waitlist.

==================================================
LEAD DELIVERY RULE
==================================================

Elite Service Partner automatically includes Direct Lead Delivery.

No separate lead toggle.

No confusion.

If Elite selected:

- Direct Lead Delivery = ON
- $49.99 per accepted lead applies
- Leads go directly to partner inbox
- Partner clicks Accept Lead
- Stripe auto-charges saved card

==================================================
BRANDING / CREATIVE FIELDS
==================================================

Elite Service Partner must upload:

- Logo
- Business display name
- Banner headline
- CTA text
- Lead destination email
- Phone
- Website

This feeds:

- Elite banner
- Trusted Service card/listing
- Lead form routing

Logo must be properly sized/cropped and not stretched.

==================================================
PUBLIC DISPLAY LOGIC
==================================================

If slot is vacant:

Show placeholder:

YOUR BUSINESS HERE  
Become the Elite Service Partner for this category in this state.  
Only one slot available.

If sold and approved:

Show business logo/banner at top of the selected category/subcategory page.

Then normal listings below.

Elite partner should also appear as the top trusted listing/card below the banner.

==================================================
RELATION TO TRUSTED PARTNER SYSTEM
==================================================

Elite Service Partner must still connect internally to the Trusted Partner system for:

- routing
- billing
- admin tracking
- leads
- Stripe
- ambassador attribution

But the public signup UX must be separate and simpler.

Do not make users go through the confusing Trusted Partner multi-service form to claim Elite.

==================================================
STRIPE FLOW
==================================================

Single Stripe checkout must include:

- Base plan
- Elite Service Partner Slot

Card saved for off-session charges.

Lead billing:

- $49.99 per accepted lead
- automatic
- no checkout link
- no manual invoice

==================================================

==================================================

- Available slots
- Sold slots
- State
- Category
- Subcategory
- Business name
- Billing status
- Creative approval status
- Leads sent
- Leads accepted
- Revenue
- Ambassador attribution

==================================================
DO NOT BREAK EXISTING SYSTEM
==================================================

Do NOT break:

- existing Trusted Partner signup
- $99 State Plan
- $499 National Plan
- Featured Placement
- Near Me Boost
- regular Direct Lead Delivery
- free resource leads
- notes/status system
- reassignment logic
- admin panel tracking

==================================================

Current problem:

Elite is buried inside Trusted Partner signup and confused with service checkboxes.

New solution:

Elite Service Partner has its own premium page and clear state/category/subcategory slot-selection flow.

This makes it easier to sell and easier for businesses to understand.
